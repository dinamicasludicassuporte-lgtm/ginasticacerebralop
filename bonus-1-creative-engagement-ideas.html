Welcome

How to Use This Bonus

1
These are idea sparks, not worksheets — there is no wrong way to try them.
2
Let your child pick the idea, not you. Choice keeps them engaged longer.
3
The 3 'try this' tips on each card are optional add-ons, not requirements.
4
It's okay to stop halfway — the goal is engagement, not a finished product.
5
Mix and match: combine an Art idea with a Storytelling idea for extra fun.

Art & Imagination Activity #1

Scribble Story

🎨

✦
Turn the scribble sideways or upside down for new ideas.

✦
