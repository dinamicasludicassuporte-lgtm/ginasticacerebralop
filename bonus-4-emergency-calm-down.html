Welcome

How to Use This Bonus

1
These are for active meltdowns — read fast, act fast, keep your own voice calm.
2
Every card takes 1-3 minutes and needs nothing but a body and a few seconds.
3
If one technique doesn't help within 2 minutes, the card tells you what to try next.
4
Talking less and doing more works best in the moment — save the conversation for after.
5
Keep this list somewhere fast to grab: the car, a bag, or on the fridge.

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #1

🚨 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

🚨
⏱ 1-2 min📍 Anywhere
Do this now:
1
Name 5 things you can see
2
Name 4 things you can touch
3
Name 3 things you can hear (then 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste)
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Wall Push-Away"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #2

🚨 Wall Push-Away

🚨
⏱ 1 min📍 Anywhere with a wall
Do this now:
1
Place both palms flat on a wall
2
Push as hard as you can for 10 seconds
3
Release, shake out your arms, repeat 3 times
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Bear Hug Squeeze"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #3

🚨 Bear Hug Squeeze

🚨
⏱ 1 min📍 Anywhere
Do this now:
1
Wrap both arms around yourself tightly
2
Squeeze hard and hold for 10 seconds
3
Release slowly, then squeeze again 2 more times
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Straw Breathing"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #4

🚨 Straw Breathing

🚨
⏱ 1-2 min📍 Anywhere
Do this now:
1
Pretend you're holding a straw
2
Breathe in through your nose for 3 counts
3
Breathe out slowly through the pretend straw for 6 counts
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "5-4-3-2-1 Grounding"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #5

🚨 Cold Water Reset

🚨
⏱ 1 min📍 Near a sink or water bottle
Do this now:
1
Run cool water over your hands, or hold a cold water bottle
2
Focus only on the feeling of the cold
3
Take 3 slow breaths while the water runs
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Bear Hug Squeeze"

🚨 Heavy Blanket Squeeze

🚨
⏱ 1-3 min📍 Anywhere with a blanket or pillow
Do this now:
1
Wrap up tightly in a blanket or hug a heavy pillow
2
Press it against your body firmly
3
Rock gently side to side for 1 minute
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Rock and Hum"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #7

🚨 Count Backwards From 20

🚨
⏱ 1 min📍 Anywhere
Do this now:
1
Say the number 20 out loud
2
Count backwards one number at a time, slowly
3
If you lose count, just start again at 20 — that's okay
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "5-4-3-2-1 Grounding"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #8

🚨 Stomp It Out

🚨
⏱ 1-2 min📍 Anywhere with floor space
Do this now:
1
Stomp your feet hard on the ground, one at a time
2
Do 10 stomps on each foot
3
Finish with 3 big, slow breaths standing still
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Wall Push-Away"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #9

🚨 Silent Scream

🚨
⏱ 30 sec - 1 min📍 Anywhere
Do this now:
1
Open your mouth as wide as it goes
2
'Scream' with no sound coming out, using all your energy
3
Close your mouth, breathe out slowly through your nose
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Stomp It Out"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #10

🚨 Push the Wall Down

🚨
⏱ 1 min📍 Anywhere with a wall
Do this now:
1
Stand facing a wall, arms straight out
2
Push like you're trying to move the wall, for 10 seconds
3
Rest 5 seconds, then push again, 3 times total
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Tight Fist Release"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #12

🚨 Tight Fist Release

🚨
⏱ 1 min📍 Anywhere
Do this now:
1
Make both hands into tight fists
2
Squeeze as hard as you can for 5 seconds
3
Open your hands wide and let your fingers go floppy
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Hand Massage"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #14

🚨 Step Outside Reset

🚨
⏱ 2-3 min📍 Anywhere with outdoor access
Do this now:
1
Step outside or near an open window if possible
2
Take 5 slow breaths of fresh air
3
Notice one thing in the sky and one thing on the ground
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Cold Water Reset"

Emergency Calm-Down Activity #15

🚨 Safe Corner Retreat

🚨
⏱ 2-3 min📍 A quiet corner or small space
Do this now:
1
Go to a quiet, low-stimulation spot
2
Sit or lie down and make yourself small and comfortable
3
Stay there until your breathing feels slow and even
💡 If it doesn't work in 2 minutes: try "Heavy Blanket Squeeze"
