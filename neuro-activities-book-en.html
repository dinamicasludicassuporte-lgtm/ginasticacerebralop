Print at A4 · One Activity Per Page · Ages 4–9
Welcome

How to Use This Book

1
Print one page at a time (or the whole book).
2
Let your child choose which activity to try — choice reduces resistance.
3
Keep sessions short: 1 to 3 pages per sitting is plenty.
4
Big lines and big pictures mean it's okay to go outside the lines — effort matters more than precision.
5
Use the Calm Breathing and Calm-Down Wheel pages any time your child feels overwhelmed.
6
Coloring pages need an image added — see the Image Placeholder Prompts list at the very end of this book.
